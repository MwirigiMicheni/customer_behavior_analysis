# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis: Insights Report

## Project Overview

This project analyzed customer shopping behavior using transactional data from **3,900 purchases** across multiple product categories. The goal was to uncover insights about customer spending patterns, customer segments, product preferences, and subscription behavior to guide strategic business decisions.

## Key Insights

### 1. Gender-Based Insights

- **Subscription gap:** No female customers in the dataset were subscribed. This finding emerged during dashboard exploration and was subsequently verified using SQL.
- **Revenue disparity:** Male customers generated approximately twice the revenue of female customers: **$157,890 vs. $75,191**.

### 2. Product Quality and Customer Satisfaction

- The average product rating was **3.75/5**, indicating potential opportunities to improve customer satisfaction.
- The five highest-rated products were:
  - Gloves: 3.86
  - Sandals: 3.84
  - Boots: 3.82
  - Hats: 3.80
  - Skirts: 3.76

### 3. Shipping-Related Insights

- **Standard shipping:** Recorded the lowest average purchase amount at **$58.46**, compared with the overall average of $59.76. However, customers using this option gave the highest average rating of **3.82/5**. 

### 4. Subscription Status

- **Average spending:** Subscribers spent slightly less per purchase than non-subscribers, averaging **$59.49 vs. $59.87**.
- **Total revenue:** Non-subscribers generated $170,436, approximately 2.7 times the $62,645 generated by subscribers. This difference may largely reflect the fact that approximately 73% of customers were not subscribed.

### 5. Customer Loyalty

- **Loyal customer segment:** A total of **3,116 customers**, approximately 80% of the customer base, were classified as loyal based on having made more than 10 previous purchases.
- **Repeat purchases and subscriptions:** The dataset contained 2,518 non-subscribed customers and 958 subscribed customers among those with more than five previous purchases, suggesting that repeat purchasing alone may not be sufficient to drive subscription enrollment.

## Recommendations

1. **Investigate the gender subscription gap.** Examine subscription offers, purchasing patterns, and potential enrollment barriers. Test targeted campaigns to understand and address differences in subscription uptake.

2. **Improve subscription value before expanding acquisition efforts.** Review subscription benefits, pricing, and usage to identify changes that could improve customer spending and justify the program's costs.

3. **Prioritize retention and repeat purchases.** Use targeted retention incentives, personalized offers, and cross-selling campaigns to encourage further purchases and increase average transaction value.

4. **Investigate product satisfaction.** Examine product-level reviews, returns, and complaints to identify underperforming products and recurring customer concerns.

5. **Evaluate shipping profitability.** Compare shipping costs, delivery satisfaction, and customer lifetime value before changing shipping prices or promoting premium delivery options.
